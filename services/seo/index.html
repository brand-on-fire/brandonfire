SEO & SEM

SEO That Earns Rankings, Not Promises

We help Marin County and Bay Area businesses get found on Google through honest, technically sound SEO. No keyword-stuffing or guaranteed-#1 gimmicks — just careful analysis, clean on-page work, local SEO, and the technical fixes that actually move rankings over time.

Get Started Book a Free Consult

Good SEO starts with understanding what your customers actually search for and how your site stacks up. We audit your pages, your site structure, and your local presence, then prioritize the changes that will earn the most visibility for the effort.

For local businesses, that usually means a well-optimized Google Business Profile, consistent business information across the web, location-relevant pages, and reviews. For everyone, it means fast, crawlable pages, sensible internal linking, and content that genuinely answers the question behind the search — increasingly important now that AI answer engines and Google’s AI overviews cite well-structured pages.

We track real outcomes — rankings, traffic, and the leads that follow — and adjust based on data, not hunches. SEO is a long game, and we’ll always be straight with you about what’s realistic and how long it takes.

FAQ

Common questions

How long until SEO works?

Technical and on-page fixes can show up within weeks, but meaningful ranking gains usually take a few months and compound over time. Anyone promising overnight #1 rankings is overselling.

Can you guarantee the #1 spot on Google?

No responsible SEO can guarantee a specific ranking — Google’s algorithm isn’t for sale. What we can do is the proven work that reliably improves visibility, and report honestly on the results.

What is AEO / AI-search optimization?

It’s structuring your content so AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) can understand and cite it — clear answers, good headings, and schema. It’s becoming a real source of traffic alongside classic search.
