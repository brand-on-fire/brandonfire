FAQ

Common questions

How much does AI consulting cost?

It depends on scope, so we don’t quote a flat number sight-unseen. Almost everyone starts with a fixed-fee readiness assessment so you know exactly what you’re spending before any building happens. From there, pilots and builds are scoped individually, and we’ll always show you the expected payoff before you commit. If a $30/month off-the-shelf tool solves your problem, we’ll tell you that instead of selling you a project.

How long until we see results?

For workflow automations and simpler tools, quick wins often show up within the first one to three months. Larger custom builds usually take longer to pay back — commonly six to nine months. We recommend giving any new AI workflow at least 90 days. Anyone promising overnight transformation is overselling.

Is my company data safe with these tools?

This is one of the first things we set up. We help you choose tools and configurations with appropriate data handling, write a simple internal usage policy covering what’s safe to share, and build guardrails into anything we deliver. A big part of our training is making sure your team understands what not to paste into a chatbot.

We’re a small business — isn’t AI really just for big companies?

No, and in some ways SMEs have an advantage: you can change a workflow next week without a committee. The catch is that generic tools rarely fit unique workflows out of the box, which is exactly the gap a consultant closes. We focus on practical, affordable wins sized for a small team.

What kind of results are realistic — will this 10x my business?

We’ll be straight with you: no. Realistic looks like saving your team a few hours a week on specific repetitive tasks and cutting routine turnaround times — think 30–50% time savings on a targeted task, not 10x on your whole business. We’d rather under-promise and over-deliver.

What if AI isn’t actually right for us right now?

Then we’ll tell you. Part of the readiness assessment is an honest “not yet” verdict when your data isn’t ready, the use case is weak, or the team isn’t bought in. We consider talking a client out of a bad project a successful engagement.

Which tools do you work with?

The practical ones: Claude, ChatGPT, and Microsoft Copilot for assistants and training; Zapier, Make, and n8n for automation; and retrieval (RAG) over your own documents so answers cite your real policies and specs. We’re tool-agnostic and pick what fits your stack and budget.

Do you work with businesses in the Bay Area in person?

Yes. We’re based in Marin County and work with small and mid-sized businesses across the North Bay and greater San Francisco Bay Area, on-site or remotely — and with clients beyond the Bay Area remotely.
